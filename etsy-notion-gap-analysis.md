# Etsy Notion → App Gap Analysis

Comparing: Etsy Workflow Framework + all 9 SOPs in Notion vs `app/etsy/page.tsx`

The biggest gaps are: a font library, a ChatGPT prompt bank, the Vela publishing workflow checklist, the primary/secondary keyword strategy hierarchy, and several practical tips scattered across the SOPs that never made it in.

## 4. PRIMARY / SECONDARY KEYWORD STRATEGY — Missing

**Source:** Marketing SOP
**Status:** The app covers keywords generally but not this framework

Notion defines a clear keyword hierarchy not in the app:

- **Primary keywords** in the title: convey the main idea ("This mug is for accountants")
- **Secondary keywords** in the title: convey additional unique positions ("Funny gifts for accountant," "Tax season gift")
- **Short tail** high-impact keywords → put in **tags** (they must be short)
- **Long tail** keywords → put in **title**
- **Short tail high-volume keywords** → put in **description** (so all listings get it)

> "To rank on the first page of a broad search you must be ranking on a specific search first"

Also missing from the app:
- "Don't put 'funny mug' — too broad. Customers won't buy on the first search anyway. RICHES IN THE NICHES."
- "Shop Section titles should be keyword-based" (e.g. "30th birthday Gifts" not just "Shirts")
- **Attributes** are "ONE OF THE BIGGEST FACTORS OF SEO" — filling them in wisely can free up tag slots (e.g. put "blue" in attribute, saves a tag)

**Recommended fix:** Expand the Marketing & SEO SOP accordion to cover this hierarchy explicitly.

## 7. TYPES OF DESIGN (TEMPLATIZED VS ONE-OFF) — Missing

**Source:** Ideas + Research SOP
**Status:** Not in app

Notion distinguishes two design types:

**Templatized:**  Designs where you interchange one word for different niches (e.g. "I stopped [ACTIVITY] to be here" → cooking, baking, reading, surfing). Fast to produce at volume.

**One-off:** Designs that can't be templatized but can have many variations. If design uses a graphic, use Midjourney to create many similar copyright-free images.

> "If you don't double down, someone else will — they'll copy it, put it on a sweatshirt in different colours and get 3× the sales"

**Recommended fix:** Add to Framework tab or Design SOP.

## 12. RESEARCH BOARD / VISION BOARD PROCESS — Missing

**Source:** Ideas + Research SOP
**Status:** Not in app

> "VISUAL RESEARCH FIRST — creativity — keyword analysis is not the most important thing"

Steps:
1. Screenshot what you like or what's popular — put in Canva or Figma whiteboard
2. Note fonts, colours, designs, graphics, words
3. Group together screenshots by design or similarity
4. Use screenshots for colour palettes in Canva
5. Include products from different but connected niches (e.g. 30th birthday → bachelorette shirts)
6. Add tags and titles from best listings — analyse them

**Recommended fix:** Add to Ideas + Research SOP or as a step in the Framework workflow phases.
