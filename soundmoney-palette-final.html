Final palette — charcoal base, three role-based accents

One base (the charcoal from option D), used everywhere. Three accents, each with its own job rather than being interchangeable — same idea as the app's own colour convention (cyan/green/amber/purple/red each mean something specific). Gold carries the wealth/history/gold content, teal carries data and explainer content, red carries urgency and inflation-alarm content. Never mix more than two accents on one thumbnail.
